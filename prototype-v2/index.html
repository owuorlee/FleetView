Four products sharing one design language, separated by environment and stake — not by screen size. Built from the materials of the Kenyan road: reflective plate white, hazard-yellow commercial plating, sodium street light, wet tarmac.

The signature

The plate block

In Kenya the registration plate is the vehicle’s identity — competitor teardown found account usernames, device names and screen titles are all the plate. So the plate becomes our universal vehicle avatar, with a status strip on its edge carrying live state without extra chrome. It appears in list rows, map callouts, alerts, receipts and the financier’s batch table.
